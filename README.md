# Privacy Policy - ClarityTask ZenDo Tool

Last updated: October 2026

ClarityTask: ZenDo Tool ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application functions regarding user data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
ClarityTask is designed as an offline task management utility tool. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) or sensitive personal data.

## 2. Local Data Storage
All tasks, categories, timer settings, and user preferences created within the application are stored locally on your device only. This data is not uploaded to any external servers.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Our application does not integrate third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or external advertising networks that harvest personal data.

## 4. Permissions
The app does not request high-risk device permissions (such as Location, Camera, Contacts, or Microphone).

## 5. Children’s Privacy
ClarityTask does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
